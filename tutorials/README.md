## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) detected the synthetic scene from its two prompts and reported `sample-sanity` `box_iou` against the drawn boxes. This notebook keeps that scene and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real labelled detection set fetched with per-file pins and its own phrase vocabulary, a corpus-level protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (AP50, AP75, per phrase) with the `predict_boxes` single-phrase-per-box scoring rule, two non-adapted baselines, a bounded fine-tuning with the upstream criterion, a per-phrase breakdown, the scene and four held-out images re-detected after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 9-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `GroundingDINOPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is the BCCD blood-cell dataset (`Shenggan/BCCD_Dataset` at the immutable commit `d272fb14cdff6e473fafeeeba32aba5f560e9e43`, MIT): 364 photographs each pinned in the carried `samples.py` by byte size and SHA-256 and fetched from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Shenggan/BCCD_Dataset/<commit>/BCCD/JPEGImages/<name>.jpg` (about 7.6 MB); a cached or fetched file is refused on any mismatch. The 4,886 boxes (the repository's Pascal VOC annotations at the same commit; two zero-area boxes dropped) travel inline, and `read_corpus` makes each photograph a record with its boxes and the phrase each is labelled with (`PROMPTS`: the class names spelled out); `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 220 / 50 / 94 and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no image (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the commit, the byte count and the licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split against the vocabulary + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `detect` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the synthetic scene (inference contract) → `grid_prior_baseline` + `majority_relabel_baseline` over the frozen model's `cell` boxes + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with the per-phrase breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `detect` + `evaluation_report` on the scene again and the example panels (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `GroundingDINOPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** every score is a sigmoid grounding score, not a calibrated probability; `detect` labels boxes under the processor's text threshold while the corpus measures use `predict_boxes` — one phrase per query, the maximum over that phrase's tokens, kept above `SCORE_FLOOR` (0.05) — and the notebook states the difference; AP50 and AP75 are measured against BCCD's labels at stated IoU bars; no acceptance threshold ships. The scene re-detected after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the grid prior, the generic-prompt relabelling and the frozen model are scored on the same 94 images as the adapted model, on both IoU bars and per phrase; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly that the frozen model sits near the baselines and that the gain is measured on three microscope phrases only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; validation (50 images) selects the epoch on mAP50 and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that images from few slides or sessions must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_IMAGE_SIDE` (16), `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (4096), `MAX_PROMPTS` (16), `MAX_PROMPT_CHARS` (48), `MAX_TEXT_TOKENS` (256), both thresholds, `SCORE_FLOOR`, `MAX_BOXES` (300), the record bounds (8..5,000) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each row of `boxes.csv` becomes a labelled box and the distinct phrases the vocabulary, and the records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected layout and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/grounding_dino_detection_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; BCCD is MIT and is not redistributed — the export records the corpus name, repository, commit, host, byte count, licence and vocabulary under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `grounding_dino_detection_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, image size, box count, count per phrase, provenance)
- `grounding_dino_detection_input_manifest.json` — the inference-contract manifest (schema, per-input observations, one recorded rejection finding)
- `grounding_dino_detection_scene_frozen.png`, `grounding_dino_detection_scene_adapted.png` — the synthetic scene with its detections before and after adaptation
- `grounding_dino_detection_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics on validation and test, the per-phrase comparison, the adaptation history
- `grounding_dino_detection_examples/` — four held-out photographs as side-by-side panels (frozen boxes, adapted boxes, labelled boxes; coloured by phrase)
- `grounding_dino_detection_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `grounding_dino_detection_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus and vocabulary, inference-contract reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime

The earlier record for the superseded `TASK-INFERENCE` carrier is kept in `../docs/release-verification.md`.
